Jaynes Appraisals

Read the provider profile · Official website

Jaynes Appraisals is a Seattle practice whose published scope includes paintings, prints, drawings, photographs, sculpture and mixed-media art. Its appraisal page describes insurance, estate, donation, damage and resale assignments, plus updates to existing appraisals.

Independent listing, not an Appraisily affiliation or guarantee. Confirm fit, fees, availability and any credential requirements directly.

What this provider’s published scope covers

The practice also covers decorative arts and books. For a fine-art assignment, identify the artists, media and number of works before requesting a scope and fee estimate.

For a written report, agree on the intended use, type of value, effective date and recipient requirements before research begins. A resale estimate, insurance value and estate valuation answer different questions.

Questions to ask before booking

  • Should these works be documented at my home or at your office?
  • Would an inventory, an update to an existing appraisal or a new report best meet my need?
  • How will edition details, signatures, provenance and any damage be recorded for my particular works?

Jaynes describes an initial discussion and estimate, documentation at the client’s home or the appraiser’s office, research and report preparation. Ask how that process applies to your collection and what access or photographs are needed before an appointment.

When a photo-based report may be enough

Appraisily is an online alternative when clear photographs and supporting records are sufficient for the assignment. Check the intended recipient’s requirements first. A local inspection may be needed when condition, attribution or inaccessible details cannot be documented remotely.
