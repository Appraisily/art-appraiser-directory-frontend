Art appraisers in Portland, Oregon

Jennifer L. Stoots, AAA

Read the provider profile · Official website

Jennifer Stoots works with photographs, photographic archives, Northwest and contemporary art. Her published CV identifies the practice in Portland; inquiries are by appointment.

Independent listing, not an Appraisily affiliation or guarantee. Confirm fit, fees and availability directly.

What the published scope covers

A photographic archive is not the same assignment as a single framed print. Stoots describes work with both, plus contemporary and Northwest art and artist estates. Her listed purposes include insurance, donation, estate, property division and damage or loss.

This page focuses on fine art. For furniture, silver or mixed household contents, use the Portland antique appraiser directory.

Questions to ask before booking

  • Does the assignment fit the provider’s photography, archive or art specialty?
  • How are archive size, inventory work and inspection included in the quote?
  • Which records are needed, and is authentication a separate task?

Agree on the intended use, type of value, effective date and recipient requirements. A resale estimate, insurance value and estate valuation answer different questions.

When an online appraisal may fit

Appraisily is a separate online option when clear photographs and records are sufficient for the assignment. Confirm the intended recipient’s requirements first. Choose local inspection when condition, attribution or inaccessible details cannot be documented remotely.
