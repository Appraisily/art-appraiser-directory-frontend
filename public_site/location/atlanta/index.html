Spalding Nix Fine Art

Spalding Nix Fine Art’s Atlanta practice offers fine-art and personal-property appraisals alongside gallery and advisory services.

Independent listing, not an Appraisily affiliation or guarantee. Confirm fit, fees and availability directly.

What the published scope covers

The published appraisal scope includes insurance, donation, estate, property division and damage assignments. The practice also offers resale walk-throughs, which its website explicitly says are not appraisals and do not include opinions of value.

This page focuses on fine art. For furniture, silver or mixed household contents, use the Atlanta antique appraiser directory.

Questions to ask before booking

  • Do you need a written appraisal or resale-oriented walk-through advice?
  • Does the provider have experience with this artist, medium and intended use?
  • What inspection, research and travel are included in the fee estimate?

Agree on the intended use, type of value, effective date and recipient requirements. A resale estimate, insurance value and estate valuation answer different questions.

When an online appraisal may fit

Appraisily is a separate online option when clear photographs and records are sufficient for the assignment. Confirm the intended recipient’s requirements first. Choose local inspection when condition, attribution or inaccessible details cannot be documented remotely.
