MIR Appraisal Services, Inc.

Read the provider profile · Official website

MIR Appraisal Services is a Chicago firm offering fine-art appraisals from Old Masters through contemporary work. Its official website describes insurance, estate, donation and equitable-distribution assignments, with on-site, office and digital inspection options.

Independent listing, not an Appraisily affiliation or guarantee. Confirm fit, fees, availability and any credential requirements directly.

What this provider’s published scope covers

MIR also works with other personal property. This page covers its fine-art services; send a list of the paintings, prints, drawings or sculpture you need assessed so the proposed assignment stays specific.

For a written report, agree on the intended use, type of value, effective date and recipient requirements before research begins. A resale estimate, insurance value and estate valuation answer different questions.

Questions to ask before booking

  • Which inspection format will document the front, reverse, labels and condition of this work?
  • Does the proposed fee include provenance research and help with an unresolved attribution?
  • What type of value and effective date will the report use for my insurance, estate or donation purpose?

Ask MIR which of its published on-site, office or digital options fits the work. If a painting is large, fragile or difficult to move, discuss an on-site visit before transporting it. If you need authentication assistance, agree on that scope separately from valuation.

When a photo-based report may be enough

Appraisily is an online alternative when clear photographs and supporting records are sufficient for the assignment. Check the intended recipient’s requirements first. A local inspection may be needed when condition, attribution or inaccessible details cannot be documented remotely.
