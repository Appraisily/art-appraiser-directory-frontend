Chicago, Illinois · Independent provider listing

MIR Appraisal Services, Inc.

MIR is a Chicago appraisal and advisory firm whose published fine-art scope extends from Old Masters to contemporary work. It serves private and corporate collectors as well as legal, insurance and financial professionals. Its website describes on-site, office and digital inspection options.

Visit MIR’s official website · Art appraisers in Chicago

Reviewed 2026-09-30 against official provider sources. Confirm current fees, availability and assignment requirements directly with MIR.

Specialties

  • Fine art from Old Masters through contemporary work
  • Paintings, prints, drawings and sculpture
  • Collection research and appraisal advisory

Services

  • Insurance coverage and damage-claim appraisals
  • Estate and charitable-contribution appraisals
  • Divorce and equitable-distribution appraisals
  • Inspection, conservation guidance and authentication-process assistance

Authentication assistance is a separate service to discuss; an appraisal report should not be treated as an authentication certificate.

Before you book

Tell MIR whether you need an insurance, estate, donation or division-of-property report. Ask which inspection format suits the work, whether the fee covers additional research, and how the intended recipient’s requirements will be handled. Its broader personal-property work is outside this fine-art listing’s scope.
