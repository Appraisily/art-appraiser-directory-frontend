Spalding Nix Fine Art

Visit the official appraisal page · Art appraisers in Atlanta

Spalding Nix Fine Art is an Atlanta gallery and art advisory with a personal-property appraisal service. Its official site describes fine-art, antique and collectible assignments in Atlanta and the Southeast, alongside acquisition and sale advice for collectors.

Reviewed 2026-10-02 against official provider sources. Confirm current fees, availability and assignment requirements directly with the practice.

Specialties

  • Fine-art valuation
  • Art within mixed personal-property collections

The gallery focuses on contemporary artists of the Southeast. Ask about experience with your particular artist and medium before commissioning an appraisal.

Services

  • Insurance and damage-or-loss appraisals
  • Donation, estate and property-division appraisals
  • Art acquisition and sale advisory

The website also offers resale-oriented walk-through evaluations. It explicitly distinguishes those from appraisals: they do not include opinions of value.

Before you book

Ask for the service you actually need: a written appraisal, resale guidance or collecting advice. Describe the artwork, intended use and inspection location. Agree on the scope and any travel costs before booking.
