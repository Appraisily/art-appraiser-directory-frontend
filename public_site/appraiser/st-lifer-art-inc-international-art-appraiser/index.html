St. Lifer Art, Inc.

New York, NY

St. Lifer Art, Inc. is a New York-based fine-art appraisal firm led by Jane St. Lifer. Its official website confirms work with private collectors, artists' estates, and corporate collections, with a focus on nineteenth-, twentieth-, and twenty-first-century art.

Provider facts reviewed against the official website on 2026-07-15. Confirm current availability and engagement terms directly with the business.

Services

Verified qualifications

The official website identifies Jane St. Lifer as an Accredited Senior Appraiser with the American Society of Appraisers and states that reports follow ASA and USPAP requirements.
