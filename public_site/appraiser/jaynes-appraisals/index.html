Jaynes Appraisals, LLC

Jaynes Appraisals is a Seattle practice led by Pamela Jaynes. Its official pages describe fine-art appraisals and collection services in the Pacific Northwest, including work with paintings, prints, photographs, sculpture and art glass. The documented process starts with a discussion of the objects and report purpose before an estimate and contract.

The practice describes documenting objects at the client’s home or office before researching the report. Confirm the visit arrangements and current delivery schedule for your project.

Before you book

Send an object list and clear photographs, then explain the report’s intended use. For art glass, include the maker’s marks and any edition information. Ask what the inspection covers, what records to gather before the visit, and whether books or decorative objects need a separate scope.
