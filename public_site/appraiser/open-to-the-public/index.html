Open to the Public

Los Angeles, CA

Open to the Public is a Los Angeles-based fine-art appraisal and consulting firm led by Kaycee Olsen. Its official website states that the practice prepares USPAP-compliant appraisals and specializes in postwar and contemporary art and photography.

Provider facts reviewed against the official website on 2026-07-15. Confirm current availability and engagement terms directly with the business.

Verified qualifications
