Brenda Simonson-Mohle

Visit Signet Art’s appraisal page · Art appraisers in Dallas

Brenda Simonson-Mohle provides fine-art appraisals through Signet Art in Dallas. The practice’s website identifies paintings, drawings, sculpture, tapestries and prints as its own areas of expertise, alongside art advisory work for individuals and collections.

Reviewed 2026-10-02 against official provider sources. Confirm current fees, availability and assignment requirements directly with the practice.

Specialties

  • Paintings, drawings and prints
  • Sculpture and tapestries

Signet Art also coordinates colleagues for other personal-property categories. That is separate from the fine-art expertise described here.

Services

  • Insurance and damage-claim appraisals
  • Estate and charitable-donation appraisals
  • Pre-purchase advice and equitable-distribution work

Agree on the report’s intended use before commissioning it; a buying consultation is not necessarily a formal appraisal.

Before you book

Use the inquiry form on the official appraisal page to explain the artwork and why you need a valuation. Signet Art says it does not appraise images by email. Ask how inspection is arranged, what documentation is needed and what the written report includes.
