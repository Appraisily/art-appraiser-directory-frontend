AFP Art Consulting, LLC

Boston, MA

AFP Art Consulting, LLC is a Boston-based fine-art appraisal and consulting firm founded by Andrea Foggle Plotkin. Its official website confirms appraisal, advisory, research, writing, collection-management, and exhibition-curation work.

Provider facts reviewed against the official website on 2026-07-15. Confirm current availability and engagement terms directly with the business.
