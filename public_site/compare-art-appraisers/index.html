Turn the comparison into an inquiry

  1. Match the artwork's medium and artist or period to the provider's stated focus.
  2. State the report's intended use and recipient. Ask what qualifications, inspection and report format the assignment requires.
  3. Agree on the scope, fee basis, travel costs and delivery schedule before booking.

Use the printable inquiry worksheet to keep those details together. For an in-person option, browse art appraisers by city.
